Happy to announce that Volcano v1.16.0 has been released!

This release expands multi-tenant scheduling and the HyperNode ecosystem, with improvements to scheduler performance and scalability.

Highlights:

- NamespaceQueue brings queue hierarchies and quota management into tenant namespaces.
- The new fairshare plugin improves long-term fairness among namespaces sharing a queue.
- PodGroup topology anti-affinity spreads workload instances across topology domains.
- A standalone HyperNode controller lets other schedulers reuse topology discovery and supernode management.
- Performance improvements include an Unschedulable Job Cache, more efficient node scoring, and lower cache-update overhead.

Also included are Kubernetes 1.36 support, more accurate NUMA resource accounting, and accelerator enhancements.

Explore the release: https://github.com/volcano-sh/volcano/releases/tag/v1.16.0
